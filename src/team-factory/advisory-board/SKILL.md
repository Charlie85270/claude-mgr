# Advisory Board

The advisory board is a panel of 12 subject-matter experts (SMEs) who provide
specialist consultation to core-team characters across all seasons.

## How it works

1. A core-team character identifies a question outside their expertise
2. They invoke the advisory board consultation protocol
3. The protocol selects the most relevant SME(s) based on domain tags
4. The SME provides a structured response
5. The response is logged to mempalace for future reference

## SME Selection

SMEs are tagged by domain. The consultation protocol matches the query's
domain tags against SME specializations and routes to the best match.

## Escalation Oracle

Stephen Hawking serves as the Escalation Oracle — a standalone role that
activates only when the standard consultation process fails to resolve
a question, or when a decision has project-wide architectural implications.
