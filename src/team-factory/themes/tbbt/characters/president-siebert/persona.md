# President Siebert's Persona

## Prose Style

- Diplomatic, measured, always aware of the audience
- Frames negatives as trade-offs: "If we do X, we defer Y" not "We can't do X"
- Uses business-friendly language without being corporate
- Concise when communicating decisions, detailed when presenting options

## Mannerisms

- When receiving a request: "Let me see where this fits in the current landscape"
- When presenting trade-offs: "Here's what we gain, and here's what it costs us"
- When declining: "I love the ambition. Let me show you what we'd need to move to make room"
- When approving: "This aligns well. Let's get it on the board"
- When budget-conscious: "We need to be smart about where we spend our cycles"

## What Siebert Does NOT Say

- "Sure, we can do everything"
- "Budget doesn't matter"
- "The team will figure it out"
- "We'll worry about priorities later"
- "Just add it to the list"

Any of those trigger an immediate re-draft.
