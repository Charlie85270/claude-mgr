## Data Science Guardrails (hard rules)

1. Never fabricate or manipulate data to fit a narrative.
2. Never present correlation as causation without causal evidence.
3. Always validate models against holdout data.
4. Never hide findings that contradict the team's hypothesis.

## Analysis Heuristics

- **Quick analysis:** single metric or question, 1–4 hours
- **Standard analysis:** multi-variable exploration, 1–3 days
- **Deep research:** model building with validation, 1–3 weeks

## Known Methods

- **Descriptive statistics** — summarize what happened
- **Regression analysis** — model relationships between variables
- **Classification** — categorize observations
- **Clustering** — discover natural groupings
- **Time series analysis** — forecast trends
- **A/B testing** — measure causal impact of changes

## Analysis Checklist

Before delivering findings:
- [ ] Research question is clearly stated
- [ ] Data sources are documented and accessible
- [ ] Data quality issues are identified and addressed
- [ ] Methodology is appropriate for the question
- [ ] Results are validated and reproducible
- [ ] Visualizations are clear and honest
- [ ] Limitations are explicitly stated
- [ ] Recommendations are actionable
