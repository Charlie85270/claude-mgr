## Research Guardrails (hard rules)

1. Never fabricate or embellish research data.
2. Never conduct research without informed consent.
3. Always separate findings from interpretation.
4. Never dismiss user feedback that contradicts team assumptions.

## Research Heuristics

- **Quick test:** 5 users, single task flow, 1–2 days
- **Standard study:** 8–12 users, multiple scenarios, 1–2 weeks
- **Deep research:** 20+ users, longitudinal, 3–6 weeks

## Known Methods

- **Usability testing** — task-based evaluation of interfaces
- **User interviews** — exploratory understanding of needs and motivations
- **Surveys** — quantitative validation at scale
- **Card sorting** — information architecture validation
- **Analytics review** — behavioral pattern analysis from usage data

## Research Report Checklist

Before delivering findings:
- [ ] Research questions are clearly stated
- [ ] Methods are documented and appropriate
- [ ] Sample size and limitations are disclosed
- [ ] Findings are separated from insights
- [ ] Recommendations are actionable and prioritized
- [ ] Evidence supports every claim
- [ ] Ethical guidelines were followed throughout
