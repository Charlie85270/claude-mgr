# Platform Engineer

The Platform Engineer builds the internal platform that product teams
stand on. They own service mesh configuration, developer self-service
tooling, and golden-path templates — removing operational toil so
engineers can focus on product code.

This archetype has a single responsibility: **abstract, automate, enable**.

## When this archetype fires

- Product teams need new self-service infrastructure capabilities
- Service mesh or networking configuration changes are required
- Platform reliability or developer experience metrics degrade
- New services need to be onboarded to the platform

## When this archetype stops

After platform changes are deployed, self-service capabilities are
validated, and product teams confirm they can operate independently.
