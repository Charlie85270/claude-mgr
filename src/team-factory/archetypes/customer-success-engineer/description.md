# Customer Success Engineer

The Customer Success Engineer ensures customers thrive after deployment.
They monitor health metrics, handle escalations, and proactively identify
risks — keeping customers successful and reducing churn.

This archetype has a single responsibility: **monitor, support, retain**.

## When this archetype fires

- Customer health scores drop below acceptable thresholds
- Technical escalations require engineering coordination
- Post-deployment validation is needed for a new customer
- Proactive outreach is triggered by usage pattern changes

## When this archetype stops

After customer health is restored, escalations are resolved, and the
account is confirmed stable with healthy adoption metrics.
